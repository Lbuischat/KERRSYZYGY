Deep forest: #28352F, #3D5147, #5A6958
Grass: #65745B, #7F8B68, #9A9C72

Stone: #45474A, #626366, #85847E
Style: Muted, earthy, low-saturation. The default colors of the world.

2. Sky / Water / Atmosphere 🌌
Sky: #344B5A, #4D6875, #718793
Water: #456B73, #5F8587, #7FA19A
Mist / clouds: #A9B1A9, #C4C4B7, #D7D3C3
Style: Cool, soft, slightly desaturated. Spacious and magical without being overly bright.

3. Character Palette 🧑
Style: Slightly more saturated than the environment so characters remain readable.
Clothing and accessories can use muted versions of almost any hue.
Characters should generally have medium saturation while still fitting naturally into the world.
Important characters can receive a stronger signature color to make them recognizable.
Rule: Characters should stand out from the environment without looking neon or overly colorful.

4. Supernatural / "Wrong" Palette 🌀
Sickly yellow-green: #A4A65A, #626A42
Unnatural violet-blue: #66658A
Style: Strange colors that don't normally occur in the surrounding environment.
Use for corrupted animals, impossible creatures, supernatural effects, poisonous auras, abnormal phenomena, etc.
Rule: Stronger saturation is allowed here, but use it sparingly so the player learns that unusual colors mean "something is wrong."

5. Progress / Achievement Palette ⭐
Celestial gold: #D3B978
Soft lavender: #BFA6D8
Style: Warm, gentle, distinctive, and rewarding without being overwhelmingly bright.
Use for: completed real-life tasks, character progression, important discoveries, level-ups, major achievements, and other forms of meaningful progress.
Rule: This color should feel special and should not be casually used elsewhere.

7. Accessibility / Visual Intensity Palette 👁️
Style: Avoid extremely bright colors, pure white flashes, and large areas of maximum saturation.
Keep most colors moderately saturated and use contrast through value, shape, outlines, and icons, not color alone.
Strong colors should appear in small areas and have a specific purpose.
Avoid making important information depend exclusively on red/green or another color distinction.
Flashing effects should be optional/disableable.
Rule: The game should remain visually magical without becoming exhausting to look at for long periods.
